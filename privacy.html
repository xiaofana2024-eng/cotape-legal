Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Cotape ("we", "the App") is a local figure-archive tool. This policy explains what data stays on your device and how permissions are used.

Data We Store on Device

  • Figure page records you create (names, series, scale, price, story, flags)
  • Photos you capture or pick for front, side, and base slots
  • Drafts, archive-pass balance, and processed App Store transaction identifiers

These items are stored locally using on-device storage. Cotape does not operate a user account backend and does not upload your archive to our servers.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you take figure photos.
  • Photo Library — used only when you choose or save images.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — used only when you dictate a story.
  • App Tracking Transparency — system prompt may appear; Cotape does not use tracking identifiers for advertising analytics in this build.
  • Notifications — system prompt may appear; Cotape does not send marketing or archive push notifications in this build.

Purchases

Archive packs are consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. We keep a local ledger of verified transaction IDs to avoid duplicate credit grants. Apple’s privacy policy also applies to payment processing.

Clearing Data

Clear All Data removes pages, media, drafts, purchase ledger entries, and remaining passes from this installation.

Contact

Support requests can be opened from the Support page inside Cotape.
